# Privacy

QDP, the Quick Desktop apps and QDP Gateway send nothing to us or to anyone
else: there are no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no
crash reports.

## What the apps send

The apps connect only to the computers you choose, directly or through your
organization's gateway if you set one, and encrypt everything they send.
While you're connected, they send that computer:

- your touches, keys, mouse and controller input;
- what you copy, so that you can paste between this device and the computer;
- your microphone's sound, only while you have turned the microphone on in
  Settings.

To find computers, the apps listen for those on your local network, which
announce their names there.

## What the apps keep

Each device keeps, for itself:

- a key that identifies it to the computers it has paired with;
- the computers you have paired with or added, and your settings;
- a sign-in token for your organization's gateway, if you use one.

They stay on the device, apart from any backup of the device you have turned
on. Removing the app deletes them, except that iPhone and iPad keep the
gateway token in the Keychain.
